Adding two integer means to combine their values to find their sum.

For example:

9 + 4 = 13

If 4 is added to 9, we can count from 9, 4 more times.
     (1)   (2)   (3)   (4)  
9... 10... 11... 12... 13...


Another example:

John has 3 apples and Jane has 4 apples. If they were to combine their apples together they would have 7 apples in total.

John's Apples: ()()() 
Jane's Apples: ()()()()
Total Apples: (1)(2)(3)(4)(5)(6)(7)
